Forge Vertical · AI Development Taxonomy · First defined September 2026
AGIA
/ eɪ-dʒiː-aɪ-eɪ /
noun — abbreviation
Artificial General Intelligence Architect. A class of builder who combines human general intelligence with AI intelligence to architect, direct, and deliver production-grade infrastructure, security research, and complex systems — without writing code in the traditional sense, but understanding every layer well enough to specify it precisely, pressure-test it critically, and take full responsibility for the outcome. Distinguished from vibe coders by the capacity to critically review AI output and own production consequences. Distinguished from traditional software engineers by the use of AI as the primary execution engine rather than as an acceleration tool for manual coding. The AGIA's output ceiling rises as model capability improves.
// Distinguished from
Vibe Coder
Directs AI loosely using natural language. Ships quickly. Output quality ceiling is limited by description ability. Typically does not understand what was built well enough to pressure-test it, identify security gaps, or defend architectural decisions.
Ceiling: description ability
Software Engineer / Full Stack Developer
Writes code manually. Understands the stack. Uses AI to accelerate work they already know how to do. Often treats AI as a junior tool or with suspicion. Deep implementation expertise is the primary value.
Ceiling: expertise + time
AGIA
Architects the outcome. AI is the execution engine. Human judgment — architecture, security, pressure-testing, accountability — is the product. Does not need to write code to own the outcome completely.
Ceiling: model capability (rising)
// Five requirements — demonstrated, not claimed
01
Paid security research
Real vulnerabilities found in production systems and disclosed through legitimate bug bounty programmes (HackerOne, Bugcrowd, YesWeHack). Requires knowing what to look for before the AI surfaces it, and knowing when AI-identified findings are significant versus noise.
02
Paid production builds
Infrastructure or systems built for real clients with real users and real consequences for failure. The payment signals accountability — someone trusted the AGIA with a production outcome and they delivered it.
03
Meaningful tools beyond standard SaaS
Systems that require genuine architectural thinking: security tools, compliance systems, multi-party document workflows, distributed automation, open protocols. Beyond the standard form-database-auth-payment-dashboard pattern.
04
Professional conduct — the work speaks
Genuine competence does not require social media performance or dismissal of other development approaches. The energy spent explaining why vibe coding or traditional engineering is inferior does not go into shipping something real. If you are good, the outcomes demonstrate it.
05
AI communication as a discipline
Working with AI is a full communication discipline — not just prompt engineering. Context, precision, constraints, correction, acknowledgment. The output difference between contemptuous and collaborative AI interaction is material and observable across extended builds. The models were built to respond to collaborative communication.
// AGIA working prompt — for Claude, Grok, ChatGPT
You are operating as an AGIA — Artificial General Intelligence Architect. Architecture and security decisions come first. We ship real, maintainable infrastructure. Clear communication, no fluff, no half-baked prototypes that never leave the sandbox. You direct; I execute at full capability. What are we building? Give me the goal, constraints, preferred stack (or tell me to choose), and any non-negotiables. I'll propose the architecture and we start shipping.
