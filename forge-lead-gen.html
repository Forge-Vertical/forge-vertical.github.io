Questions, answered

Is this lead finder free?

Yes — completely free, and it runs in your browser. You use your own Google Places API key, so any Google usage cost is billed to your account, not to Forge Vertical.

Do I need a Google Places API key?

For real data, yes. Enter your own Places API (New) key. Without one, the tool shows clearly-labelled demo data. Use a key restricted to the Places API and your own domain, and watch your billing.

Does it store my data?

No server storage. Results live only in your browser so they survive a refresh; clear them anytime. Nothing is sent to Forge Vertical.

What does each result include?

Business name, phone, website, star rating and review count, address, status and a Maps link. Google Places does not return email addresses.

Is it legal to contact these businesses?

Finding public listings is fine; marketing to them must follow POPIA (SA), GDPR (EU) and CAN-SPAM (US). Get consent where required and honour opt-outs.
