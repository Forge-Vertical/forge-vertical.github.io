// Background

How I got here

I am self-taught. No computer science degree — primary schooling to Grade 6, then a path through collision repair training at Lance's Panel and Spray, a TEFL Level 5 certification, teaching English in China, and working as a safety crew member on Fear Factor India productions in Cape Town. I taught myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, and C++ via Udemy, then kept building.

I founded Prinymity in 2018 — custom PC builds, small business websites, privacy and anonymity focus. Prinymity evolved into Forge Vertical. TripSpace Global came next. I treat AI as a co-worker rather than a tool — directing it, reviewing its output, and shipping production systems as a solo operator. I am married to Micaela, who runs SiteBuild Studio.
