Our Mission

The Maintenance Plumbing and Entrepreneurial Skills Development program is a targeted initiative offering relevant practical courses, theoretical knowledge, and apprenticeship pathways.

By bridging the skills gap in essential maintenance plumbing, we prepare participants for sustainable self-employment, enterprise creation, and lasting community resilience.

Why Maintenance Plumbing?

Plumbing is essential for maintaining sanitation, water infrastructure, and functional operations across residential, commercial, and industrial facilities. Our curriculum ensures preventive maintenance that safeguards vital systems while establishing local employment avenues.

Program Goals

01. PROVIDE

Practical plumbing maintenance skills to empower community members and restore long-term dignity.

02. DEVELOP

Personal and technical capacity through structured hands-on vocational education and mentoring.

03. INCREASE

Direct employability and entrepreneurial opportunities across maintenance plumbing trades.

04. IMPROVE

Community awareness around water and land conservation, emphasizing sustainable infrastructure care.

05. ENCOURAGE

Self-sufficiency, economic resilience, and active contribution to the broader South African economy.
