Why This Program Exists

Skills that restore dignity — and pay the bills.

Plumbing keeps homes, schools and businesses running. It's also a trade with steady demand and a low barrier to entry — which makes it a direct route out of unemployment when it's taught properly, with mentorship attached. That's the gap Seriti fills.

Restore Dignity Through Skill

Give people practical plumbing maintenance skills they can use immediately to support themselves and their households.

Grow Capacity, Not Just a Certificate

Develop each participant's understanding and confidence through structured education and hands-on experience, not theory alone.

Open Real Employment Pathways

Increase employability in plumbing maintenance specifically, and in the broader trades and small-business economy.

Build Water & Land Awareness

Every graduate leaves understanding water conservation and responsible land use — skills the whole community benefits from.

The Training Pipeline

From first fitting to first client.

The Maintenance Plumbing and Entrepreneurial Skills Development program runs as one connected line — each stage feeds directly into the next, the same way a working system does.

01

Learn the Trade

Core maintenance plumbing instruction — real tools, real fittings, real household and commercial systems.

02

Apprentice & Mentor

Supervised, hands-on placements pair theory with practical experience under experienced tradespeople.

03

Business Skills

Entrepreneurship training so graduates can quote, invoice, and run their own plumbing maintenance work.

04

Work & Earn

Participants move into employment or start independent plumbing maintenance businesses in their community.
