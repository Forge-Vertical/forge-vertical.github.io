Services

Bookkeeping, payroll and compliance, built to work together

Four services, one team — so nothing falls between your bookkeeper, your payroll and your SARS submissions. Here's what each one actually covers.

01 / Bookkeeping

Clean, current financial records

The foundation everything else is built on. We keep your books accurate and up to date so you always know where the business actually stands — not where it stood three months ago.

  • Monthly or weekly transaction processing and bank reconciliations
  • Management accounts you can actually read and act on
  • Cash flow and debtor/creditor tracking
  • Clean handover-ready records for your auditor or accountant
  • Set-up and ongoing support on Xero or Sage
02 / Payroll

Payroll that runs on time, every time

Payslips, statutory filings and employee changes handled on a fixed schedule, for businesses of any size — from a two-person team to a growing staff complement across provinces.

  • Monthly payroll processing and payslip distribution
  • PAYE, UIF and SDL calculations and submissions
  • Statutory filings (EMP201, EMP501) managed on deadline
  • New hire, leave and termination administration
  • COIDA / Workmen's Compensation returns
03 / Tax compliance

SARS compliance, handled by people who keep up with it

Tax legislation and SARS requirements change often. Our team tracks the compliance calendar so you don't have to, and steps in the moment SARS sends a letter.

  • Provisional and annual company and individual tax returns
  • VAT registration, calculation and bi-monthly submissions
  • SARS correspondence, queries and dispute support
  • Tax clearance certificate applications
  • Tax planning aligned to your financial year-end
04 / Business registrations

Starting a business the right way, from day one

Company registrations handled efficiently and correctly — so your new entity starts with its finances, tax and compliance properly structured, not patched together after the fact.

  • CIPC company registration (Pty Ltd, NPC)
  • SARS income tax and VAT registration
  • UIF and Compensation Fund registration
  • B-BBEE affidavit guidance
  • Bank account opening support documentation
