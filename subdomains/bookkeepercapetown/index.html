Questions, answered

The things business owners usually ask before switching bookkeepers.

What does a bookkeeper do?
A bookkeeper manages your business's financial records, including tracking income and expenses, reconciling bank accounts, preparing financial reports, and ensuring compliance with tax regulations.
Why do I need a bookkeeper for my small business?
A bookkeeper ensures your financial records are accurate, up-to-date, and compliant with laws, giving you more time to focus on growing your business. They also provide insights into your financial health.
What's the difference between a bookkeeper and an accountant?
A bookkeeper focuses on daily financial transactions, while an accountant provides strategic advice, prepares tax returns, and offers financial analysis based on bookkeeping data.
How often will I need bookkeeping services?
This depends on your business size and needs. Most businesses require monthly services, but larger enterprises may need weekly or even daily support.
Can you help me with payroll?
Yes, we offer payroll services, including calculating salaries, deductions, and taxes, and generating payslips for your employees.
Do you assist with SARS and tax compliance?
Yes, we provide SARS correspondence, tax registrations, eFiling submissions, and assistance with tax returns and directives.
Can you help me register my business?
Yes, we handle company registrations, ensuring your business is compliant and ready to operate legally.
What industries do you work with?
We serve small to medium-sized businesses across various industries, tailoring our services to meet their specific needs.
Do you offer management account reporting?
Yes, we provide monthly management accounts and reports to help you track performance and make informed decisions.
Can you assist with legal documentation, like wills or cost drafting?
Yes, we offer estate planning, will drafting, and preparation of legal cost documents to safeguard your financial future.
What software do you use for bookkeeping?
We work with leading accounting software like Xero, QuickBooks, and Pastel, ensuring accuracy and efficiency in managing your finances.
How much do bookkeeping services cost?
Our pricing depends on the size of your business and the scope of work. Contact us for a consultation to discuss your needs and get a personalized quote.
Can I outsource all my financial tasks to you?
Yes, we provide comprehensive outsourcing services, including bookkeeping, payroll, tax compliance, and financial reporting.
How do I get started with Bookkeepers SA?
It's simple! Book a consultation with us, and we'll discuss your needs, create a tailored plan, and provide ongoing support to manage your finances efficiently.
