Questions, answered

The things business owners usually ask before switching bookkeepers.

Can you take over mid-way through a tax year?
Yes. We regularly onboard businesses mid-year — we'll review your existing records, clean up anything that needs it, and pick up your bookkeeping and SARS submissions from wherever you currently stand.
Do you work with Xero and Sage, or only your own system?
Both. Our team is Xero Advisor Certified and Sage accredited, so we can work inside the accounting software you already use, or set you up on one if you don't have a preference yet.
What's included in your monthly bookkeeping fee?
Pricing depends on transaction volume and whether payroll or VAT submissions are included. We'll give you a clear, fixed monthly quote after an initial consultation — no hourly surprises.
Can you register a new company and handle its books from day one?
Yes — company registration, tax and VAT setup, and ongoing bookkeeping and payroll can all run through us, so a new business starts with its finances properly structured from the outset.
Do I need to be in Cape Town or Johannesburg to work with you?
No. While we have offices in both cities, most client communication happens by phone, email and video call, so businesses anywhere in South Africa can work with us.
