Professionally accredited, and certified on the software you already use

Our team holds the professional body memberships and software certifications that back up the work we do — so you're not just trusting a bookkeeper, you're trusting a regulated, verified standard of work.

Professional & software accreditations

Four accreditations our team is held to, and certified on.

CIBA — Chartered Institute for Business Accountants

Our bookkeepers and accountants are registered with CIBA, a recognised South African professional body for business accountants, holding us to a defined code of conduct and ongoing professional development.

SAIT — South African Institute of Tax Professionals

Our tax practitioners are registered with SAIT, the professional body recognised by SARS and the South African Revenue Service for tax practitioner registration and oversight.

Xero Advisor Certified

Our team is certified on Xero, the cloud accounting platform, so clients running their books on Xero get advice and support from people who know the software inside out.

Sage certified

We're also certified on Sage, giving clients already using Sage Business Cloud Accounting or Sage Payroll a team that can work directly inside their existing system.
