Common questions

Quick answers.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and what the site needs to do. You get a fixed quote in writing before any work starts, so you know the price up front. There is no surprise bill later.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The code, content, domain and hosting accounts are handed over to you. There is no lock-in and no licence fee to keep your site online.

Will my website show up on Google?

Nobody can honestly promise a position on Google. What we do is build the site properly from day one: fast, mobile-friendly, with clear page titles and the technical setup Google and AI search tools look for. That gives your business the best chance of being found.

How long does it take?

It depends mostly on how quickly your photos, text and details reach us. We agree the dates in the quote, and a site can go live while you are still finding extra photos. They can be added afterwards.

Do you only work in Cape Town?

We are based in Cape Town and work with local businesses in person or on WhatsApp. We also work with clients elsewhere in South Africa and abroad.

What happens after the site is live?

You receive a handover with everything you need. If you want changes later, we quote them or handle them under a support plan, so you always know the cost before we start.
