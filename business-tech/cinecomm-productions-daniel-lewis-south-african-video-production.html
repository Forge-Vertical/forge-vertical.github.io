// Chapter 01 — The business

Video for businesses that want more customers

Plenty of small businesses know they should be using video, and few know where to start. That is the gap CineComm Productions works in. Founded by Daniel Lewis, the company makes video meant to do a job for the business: tell its story, show what it sells, and give people a reason to get in touch.

CineComm describes itself as a video production agency built around brand storytelling, marketing strategy and measurable results. On its channel, it makes a bold promise: it says it will convert 80% of your leads into sales using video. That is the company's own claim, and a big one, but it tells you how seriously it takes the link between a video and a sale.

// Chapter 02 — The range

What CineComm makes

The list on its website is long. It covers:

  • Brand and corporate videos, to say who a business is and what it stands for.
  • Social media and promotional videos, built for the feeds where customers spend their time.
  • Product demo, explainer and tutorial videos, to show people how something works before they buy.
  • Testimonial and documentary videos, where customers and real stories do the talking.
  • Event coverage, including opening and recap videos.
  • Wedding videos and videography, for the days people want to keep.
  • Animated and infographic videos, post-production editing and customer support videos.
  • Cinematography workshops, for people who want to learn the craft.

Alongside the filming, CineComm also talks about lead generation and digital marketing, because a video that nobody sees does not help anyone.

// Chapter 03 — The clients

Who has worked with him

CineComm's website shows a wall of client logos, among them Eskom, Osmans, Property Academy, Mpact, Scan Global Logistics, Luxe Fashion Group and Dona Crowley Events. The site does not say what was made for each, so we will not guess. For a business owner deciding who to trust with something as visible as a brand film, that kind of feedback matters as much as the showreel.

// Chapter 04 — Why it matters

Why South African businesses are turning to video

A photo and a paragraph can only say so much. A short video can show your premises, introduce the person behind the business, and let a customer see your product in use, all in under a minute. For a small business competing with bigger names, that closeness is a real advantage.

It also travels. The same video can sit on your website, go out on social media, play at an event and be sent to a customer who is thinking it over. One piece of work, used in many places.

The hard part is making it well. Good video takes planning, a good eye, sound that is easy to hear and editing that respects the viewer's time. That is the craft CineComm is selling, and Daniel is the person behind it.
