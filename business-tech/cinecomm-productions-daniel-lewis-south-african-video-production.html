Who is Daniel Lewis? Daniel Lewis is the founder of CineComm Productions, a video production agency that makes brand films, social media content, product demos, event coverage and wedding videos for businesses and individuals. Born in Johannesburg and now living in Florida, he works with clients in both South Africa and the United States.

// Chapter 01 — The founder

From Johannesburg to Florida, with a camera

Daniel Lewis is from Johannesburg. Today he lives in Florida and runs CineComm Productions from there, with a client list that reaches across two countries. Whether the project is a South African brand that needs a film, or a business in the United States that wants to show what it does, the approach is the same: tell the story well, and make it work for the business.

That focus on results runs through everything he makes. CineComm builds video around brand storytelling, marketing strategy and a simple aim: turn the people who watch into people who get in touch, and the people who get in touch into customers.

// Chapter 02 — The work

What CineComm makes

CineComm covers the full range of video a business is likely to need:

  • Brand and corporate videos, to say who a business is and what it stands for.
  • Social media and promotional videos, built for the feeds where customers spend their time.
  • Product demos, explainers and tutorials, to show people how something works before they buy.
  • Testimonial and documentary videos, where real customers and real stories do the talking.
  • Event coverage, including opening and recap videos.
  • Wedding videos, for the days people want to keep.
  • Animated and infographic videos, post-production editing and customer support videos.
  • Cinematography workshops, for people who want to learn the craft.

Alongside the filming, CineComm helps with lead generation and digital marketing, so the video is seen by the right people and does its job once it is made.

// Chapter 03 — The clients

Who he has worked with

Daniel has worked with a long list of organisations, among them Eskom, Osmans, Property Academy, Mpact, Scan Global Logistics, Luxe Fashion Group and Dona Crowley Events.

The people who have worked with him describe him as hardworking, upbeat and professional. For a business owner choosing someone to put their brand on camera, those are exactly the qualities you want in the person who puts your brand on camera.

// Chapter 04 — Why video

A minute of video can do what a page of text cannot

A short video can show your premises, introduce the person behind the business, and let a customer see your product in use, all in under a minute. For a smaller business competing with bigger names, that closeness is a real advantage.

It also travels. The same video can sit on a website, go out on social media, play at an event and be sent to a customer who is thinking it over. One piece of work, used in many places. Making it well takes planning, a good eye, clear sound and editing that respects the viewer's time, and that is the craft Daniel brings to every project.
