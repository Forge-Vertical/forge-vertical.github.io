Atelier OS:
the business OS that fits how you work.

Quotes, invoices, payments, customers, branches and plugins in one place. Built in Cape Town for South African SMEs.

Jarrit Hosking
Forge Vertical · Cape Town · November 19, 2026
5 min read

What is Atelier OS? Atelier OS is a Business OS for South African SMEs. You build a quote, send it by email, the customer accepts it from a link, one click turns it into an invoice, and they pay by card, Yoco link, bank transfer or QR code. Customers, items, expenses, branches and an audit log sit in the same place. You start with what you need, on a free plan if you like, and add more as you grow.

01 / The idea

Software that fits how you work

Most business software expects you to work the way it was built. Atelier works the other way round. Start with the tools you need, add capabilities as your business grows, and build a workspace around how you actually work. The same platform serves a contractor, a mechanic, a consultant and a shop owner. Atelier is not built to compete with other tools. It is an evolving system for every industry, and it works alongside the accounting software you already use.

02 / Quotes and invoices

From quote to paid, without the back-and-forth

  1. Build a quote. Add line items by hand or pull them from your catalog, then send it by email in minutes.
  2. Customer accepts. No account needed. They review the quote and accept or decline from a link, on their phone.
  3. Convert to invoice. One click turns the accepted quote into an invoice, and the numbers carry across.
  4. Get paid. Card through PayFast, a Yoco link, bank transfer or a QR code, whichever your business has set up.

You can also download a PDF of any quote or invoice. Once a customer has paid, the invoice shows as paid on your dashboard, so you are not checking a bank app to find out.

Live 03 / Your customers

A better experience on their side too

Customers get a professional link where they can review a quote, accept or decline it, view invoices and pay, straight from their phone. There is no complicated sign-up. They know exactly where things stand, and your business looks more professional for it.

Live 04 / Running the business

Customers, items, expenses and reports

Keep a customer list, track inventory items with low-stock alerts, log expenses, and run reports across all of it. Import your existing contacts by CSV instead of typing them again, and download reports for your bookkeeper. Your dashboard shows what you were paid this month, what is outstanding and how many quotes you have sent. An audit log records all activity, so you can see what was done and when.

Live 05 / Branches and franchises

One account. Multiple businesses. Complete visibility.

Run more than one branch or franchise location from a single admin account, with billing handled per branch. Each business or branch operates within its own workspace, which suits entrepreneurs, business groups and franchise operators.

// Questions answered

What is Atelier OS?

Atelier OS is a Business OS for South African SMEs, built by Forge Vertical in Cape Town. You start with the tools you need, such as quotes, invoices and payments, and add capabilities as your business grows, from customers, inventory and expenses to branches, franchises and Store plugins.

Who is Atelier OS for?

Construction and contractors, automotive businesses, professional services, retail and suppliers, and franchise operators. These are four ways of using the same platform.

What does Atelier OS cost?

Atelier has three plans. Free is R0 and includes 50 quotes and 6 invoices a month, with Atelier branding on documents. Individual is R599 a month with unlimited quotes and invoices, your own branding and Store plugins. Franchise is R900 a month plus R900 a month per branch, with unlimited quotes and invoices, a franchise admin dashboard and guided data migration. Store plugins are priced separately, from free to R150 a month, with SMS Notifications from R200 a month. You can try the free plan first.

Can my customers pay online?

Yes. Invoices can be paid by card through PayFast, by Yoco link, by bank transfer or by scanning a QR code. The customer portal shows whichever options your business has set up, and a paid invoice shows as paid on your dashboard.

Can I run more than one branch or business?

Yes. Atelier lets you run more than one branch or franchise location from a single admin account, with billing handled per branch.

Which plugins are available?

Low Stock Alerts and Coupon are free. VIN Scanner, Property Link Import and Booking Calendar are R150 a month each, Email Credits are from R150 a month and SMS Notifications are from R200 a month. Plugins can also be requested and built around how your business works, and charges may apply depending on scope. Once a plugin is in the Store, it is available to every Atelier business, and each one is free, a subscription or a once-off payment.
