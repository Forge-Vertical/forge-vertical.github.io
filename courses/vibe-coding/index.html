What you will learn — all 9 modules
Written. Practical. No padding. Every module covers something that has broken a real project.
01
What vibe coding actually is — and what it is not
The honest definition. Where it works brilliantly and where it will burn you. The difference between shipping a landing page and shipping a payment system. Setting your expectations correctly before you spend a week building the wrong thing.
02
Communicating with AI correctly — the slop problem
AI slop is a communication problem, not an AI problem. The exact same relationship rules that apply between humans apply here — vague brief, vague output. This module gives you the prompting discipline that separates builders who ship from builders who restart constantly.
03
Before you build — research, requirements, and knowing your laws
The thinking that happens before the first prompt is where good products start. What does it do. Who uses it. What data does it hold. What countries does it operate in. What laws apply. If you cannot answer these, you are not ready to build yet.
04
Security from the first prompt — not the last
Input validation, parameterised queries, rate limiting, CSRF protection. What to add to every project prompt. The specific security instruction template that catches the majority of vulnerabilities before they exist. Cloudflare setup — the eight settings that matter on the free tier.
05
Race conditions — the bug you do not know you have
Two users clicking simultaneously. A payment button clicked twice. Inventory sold to two buyers. Race conditions are the most common invisible bug in vibe-coded projects. What they are, how to find them, and what to ask the AI to add to prevent them.
06
Payments, money, and when to get help
PCI-DSS in plain language. Why you never store card numbers. How to use Stripe, PayFast, or PayPal correctly. Idempotency keys. Webhook verification. The specific questions to ask the AI when building any payment flow. And when to bring in a professional rather than prompt your way through it.
07
Privacy law, cookie consent, and POPIA for South African builders
POPIA. GDPR. CCPA. What each requires. The cookie consent implementation that loads analytics only after the user accepts. The privacy policy template checklist. What "I collect email addresses" actually means legally in South Africa. Written in plain language, not legalese.
08
Royalty-free assets, testing, and the responsibility rule
Using a Google Images result on a commercial site is copyright infringement. The safe sources — Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash, and AI-generated images. The pre-launch test checklist. And the rule that every vibe coder needs tattooed somewhere visible: "Claude built it" is not a legal defence.

GPT-6 Astra. Fable 5.1. The leap from 2022 to 2026 in four years. What the next four years look like. Dario Amodei's honest prediction.
