// Questions

Questions people ask

What does Forge Vertical build?

Forge Vertical builds custom management systems, business operating systems, client and staff portals, SaaS platforms, role-based data access with audit trails, and AI integrations. We also build websites. Everything is built around how your business works, and you own it.

How long does a custom system take to build?

Most platforms take 6 to 10 weeks from the signed quote and deposit to the live system. Smaller tools can be quicker. You get a written timeline with the quote.

Do you build multi-tenant software?

Yes. Multi-tenant means many customers or branches use one platform, but each only ever sees its own data. It is the basis of both SaaS products and franchise systems.

What happens after the system goes live?

You get documentation and a walkthrough, and the system is yours. If you want ongoing monitoring, fixes and changes, a monthly retainer is quoted with the build. It is optional.

Can you add AI to a system I already have?

Often yes, if the system has a way to connect to it. We will look at what you run today and say plainly whether adding to it, or replacing it, makes more sense.

Where is Forge Vertical based?

Forge Vertical is based in Cape Town, South Africa, and builds for businesses across South Africa and internationally. Work is delivered online, with video or in-person meetings in the Cape Town area.
