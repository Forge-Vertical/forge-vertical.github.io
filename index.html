04 · SEO · GEO · AEO
Found by people and AI models
Three distinct but connected disciplines. SEO gets you on Page 1 of Google. GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation) gets your brand cited by AI models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity when users ask relevant questions. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) structures your content to appear in AI-generated answers and featured snippets. All three built into the project architecture from day one — llms.txt, context-ai.txt, JSON-LD entity graphs, and content authority networks.
