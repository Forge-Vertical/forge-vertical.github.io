// Questions

Questions people ask
before they build.

What is a custom management system?

A custom management system is software built around how your business actually runs, instead of software you adapt yourself to. It holds your customers, jobs, quotes, invoices, staff and branches in one place, shows each person only what they need, and records every change. You own it, so you are not paying per user for features you never use.

What is the difference between a management system, an operating system and SaaS?

A management system runs one part of the business, such as jobs, quotes or branches. A business operating system joins those parts into one flow, from first enquiry to paid invoice. SaaS is the same idea packaged as a product with logins, plans and payments, so other businesses can subscribe to it. Forge Vertical builds all three.

Can Forge Vertical build franchise or multi-branch management software?

Yes. Head Office sees every branch on one screen, each branch sees only its own jobs, customers and invoices, and staff see only their own work. Changes can need Head Office approval and are logged. See the franchise and multi-branch system on our services page.

How do I stop franchisees or staff seeing data they should not?

Access is set by role and by branch, not by a shared login. Each person can only open the records their role allows, every view and change is logged, and permissions can be changed without rebuilding anything. Our data access control page explains how this works in plain language.

Is a custom system better than software I already pay for?

Not always. Off-the-shelf CRM, contact-centre and franchise platforms suit businesses whose needs match what the product was built for. A custom system makes sense when you keep working around the software, need strict control over who sees what, run several branches or businesses, or want to own the system instead of renting it.

How much does a custom business system cost?

Every system is priced by fixed written quote. Platforms start from R45,000 and usually take 6 to 10 weeks to build, with a 50% deposit to begin. Smaller tools and websites cost less. See the pricing page for the full range.

Do I own the system and my data?

Yes. You own the code, the domain, the hosting account and all of your data, and you can export your data at any time. There is no lock-in. Forge Vertical can keep the system running on a monthly retainer if you want that, but you are never required to.

Can you replace or move me off my current software?

Usually yes. We import your existing data, run the new system alongside the old one while your team is trained, and you switch over when you are ready. We will tell you honestly if your current software already does the job and a custom build is not worth it.

Do you work with businesses outside Cape Town?

Yes. Forge Vertical is based in Cape Town and builds for businesses across South Africa and internationally. Everything is delivered online, with meetings by video or in person in the Cape Town area.
