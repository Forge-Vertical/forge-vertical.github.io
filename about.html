// The story

From small-business sites
to systems that run the business.

Jarrit started out building custom PCs and small-business websites in Cape Town, with a focus on privacy. Before that he trained in the collision repair trade, so he knows what a busy workshop, a quote and a job card really look like. He taught himself to code and kept moving deeper: from websites, to portals, to full management systems and SaaS platforms.

That is why Forge Vertical talks about systems, not just websites. Most business problems are not about how a page looks. They are about who can see what, who changed what, and whether the owner knows how the day went.

// Questions

About Forge Vertical

Who is behind Forge Vertical?

Forge Vertical is run by Jarrit Hosking, a software architect based in Cape Town. He designs, builds and runs every system himself, with AI as a working partner.

Is Forge Vertical a big agency?

No. It is a one-person studio, which means the person you brief is the person who builds your system. There are no account managers and no hand-offs.

What kind of businesses does Forge Vertical work with?

Franchises, multi-branch groups, owner-operated businesses with several divisions, and founders building a SaaS product. Industries so far include automotive, hospitality, trades, education and travel.

Where is Forge Vertical based?

Cape Town, South Africa. Forge Vertical works with businesses across South Africa and internationally, mostly online.

Does Forge Vertical do security work?

Yes. Jarrit is an active security researcher on HackerOne, Bugcrowd and YesWeHack, and that way of thinking shapes how access control and audit trails are designed in every system.
