for property, rentals and businesses for sale.

Stays, homes for sale, short-term and long-term rentals, and accommodation businesses for sale, all in one place. What it is, how it works and who it suits.

What is TripSpace Global now? It is a free-to-list marketplace for holiday places and property. On one site you can list or find a place to stay, a home or holiday home for sale, a short-term or long-term rental, and an accommodation business for sale. Owners, agents and brokers can all list, and there is no commission on a sale because TripSpace is not the agent. If something breaks, email support@tripspaceglobal.com and it is fixed within 24 hours.

01 / What changed

From a place to stay to a place for property

TripSpace Global began as a booking site for holiday accommodation. It still is. What has changed is everything around it. Anyone who owns a place, rents a place, wants to sell a place or wants to buy a running accommodation business can now do that in the same spot, with one seller profile and no listing fee.

The idea is simple. People who think about holiday places also think about property: the holiday home they might buy one day, the flat they could rent out, the guesthouse someone wants to hand over. Those are the same people looking for the same kind of place. It makes sense to put them in one room.

03 / Free means free

What “free to list” actually means

Listing on TripSpace Global costs nothing today. There is no monthly fee for putting a property, rental or business in front of buyers, and TripSpace takes no commission on a sale or a rental, because the deal happens between you and the other person.

An honest note. The platform is young and the model can change as it grows. Free today is not a promise about every future year. Check tripspaceglobal.com for the current terms before you list.
04 / Selling

Selling a property outright

If you own a home or holiday home and want to sell, create a seller profile, choose whether you are an owner or an agent, and add the property. Listings that get enquiries tend to have the same few things:

  • Clear photos of the outside, the main rooms and the view or setting.
  • The town, province and country set correctly, so the listing shows up in the right searches.
  • A plain description of the size, rooms, parking, security and what is included in the sale.
  • An asking price, or a clear statement that you want offers.
  • An honest line on why it suits a buyer, and what a buyer should know.

Buyers message you through TripSpace. If you also want calls, WhatsApp or email, add those details to your profile. They are only shown to signed-in visitors who tap Contact seller, and you can leave any box empty to keep it private.

05 / Selling a business

Selling an accommodation business

Selling a guesthouse, B&B or holiday-rental business is different from selling a house. The buyer is buying income, reputation and a system as well as bricks. The Take over a stay option exists for that.

A good business listing covers:

  • What is included: building or lease, furniture, equipment, bookings calendar, reviews, website, social accounts and phone numbers.
  • How it trades: number of rooms or units, the busy and quiet seasons, and how guests find it today.
  • Where it is and what the area offers guests.
  • The asking price and the reason for selling.
  • What you will share with a serious buyer once you have spoken, such as figures and booking history.

Keep the first listing honest and simple. Serious buyers will ask for more, and they will check everything with their own advisers before they sign. That is normal, and it is a good sign when they do.

07 / For buyers

If you are looking, not listing

You do not need an account to look. Pick Stay, Buy, Take over a stay or Rent on the home page, search by town or country, and message a seller through TripSpace when something fits.

A few sensible habits, whatever site you use:

  • Visit the property, or send someone you trust, before you commit money.
  • Ask for proof of ownership, and use an attorney or conveyancer for any sale.
  • For a business, ask to see real booking and trading records, and talk to your own adviser.
  • Keep all early conversation inside TripSpace until you are comfortable.

TripSpace Global is a listing platform. It is not an estate agent, and it does not guarantee any individual listing or seller, so the checking is yours to do.

09 / Why this direction

One place for holiday places and property

Most travel sites stop at the booking. Most property sites stop at the sale. People live between the two: they holiday somewhere, fall for it, and start wondering about a place of their own. A single site that covers stays, rentals, property and accommodation businesses fits how people actually think about a place.

It is early, and it will grow in steps. If you own a holiday place, rent one out, want to sell, or are looking for something to take over, listing is free and takes a few minutes.

// Questions answered

What is TripSpace Global?

TripSpace Global (tripspaceglobal.com) is an online marketplace for holiday places and property. You can find places to stay, homes and holiday homes for sale, short-term and long-term rentals, and accommodation businesses for sale, all on one site. It was built by Jarrit Hosking of Forge Vertical in Cape Town and is open to listings from South Africa and elsewhere.

Is it free to list on TripSpace Global?

Yes. Listing is free today, whether you are an owner, an agent or a broker, and whether you are listing a stay, a property for sale, a rental or an accommodation business for sale. The platform may change as it grows, so check the site for the current terms before you list.

What can I list on TripSpace Global?

Four kinds of listing: a place to stay, a property or holiday home for sale, a property to rent (short-term or long-term), and an accommodation business for sale, which is listed under the Take over a stay option. One seller profile can carry several properties.

Can I sell my guesthouse, B&B or holiday-home business on TripSpace Global?

Yes. The Take over a stay option is for owners who want to sell a running accommodation business, not just the building. A good listing says what is included (furniture, bookings calendar, reviews, website, social accounts), the location, the asking price and why you are selling. Serious buyers will still want to check the details with you and their own advisers before anything is signed.

Can agents and brokers list on TripSpace Global?

Yes. When you create a seller profile you choose whether you are an owner or an agent/broker. Your name, and your company name if you add one, shows on your listings. Phone, WhatsApp and email are optional and are only shown to signed-in visitors who tap Contact seller. Buyers can always message you through TripSpace.

Does TripSpace Global handle the sale or take a commission?

No. TripSpace Global is a place to list and find properties and businesses. It does not act as the estate agent, does not take a cut of a sale and does not hold or move the money. Price, terms and the legal transfer are agreed directly between buyer and seller, normally with an attorney or conveyancer involved.

How do I contact TripSpace Global if I find a bug or have a problem?

Email support@tripspaceglobal.com. Jarrit Hosking handles support personally and aims to resolve bugs and issues within 24 hours. Include the page address, what you expected to happen and what happened instead, and a screenshot if you can.

Is TripSpace Global only for South Africa?

No. Listings are set by town, province and country, so a seller anywhere can list. The platform started with a focus on South Africa and Cape Town, and Cape Town accommodation is a strong part of it today.

Can I rent out a property long-term as well as short-term on TripSpace Global?

Yes. Rentals are not limited to holiday stays. You can list a property for short-term rental, long-term rental, or sell it outright, all from the same seller profile.
